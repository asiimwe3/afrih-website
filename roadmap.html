10-Year Roadmap

Milestone-driven growth: Prove, Institutionalize, Scale, Recycle Capital

Strategic Timeline

Year 1

Formation + Pilots

Parent structure, governance, first capital, first 3-5 pilot projects.

Years 2-3

Proof + Fundraising Engine

Operating revenue, investor pipeline, repeatable project model, dedicated sector subsidiaries.

Years 3-5

Sector Expansion

Multiple subsidiaries, JV pipeline, DFI relationships, project-finance templates.

Years 5-7

Regional Scale

Country platforms, larger funds, project-finance capability, cross-border trade.

Years 7-10

Institutional Scale

Capital markets access, major infrastructure portfolio, mature governance, capital recycling.
