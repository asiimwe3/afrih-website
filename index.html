About AFRIH

Turning a Pan-African vision into a repeatable capital-and-enterprise machine.

Our Vision

Build a connected African economic platform that mobilizes capital and develops productive enterprises across the continent.

Our Mission

Create investable businesses and infrastructure, connect African markets, strengthen local value chains, and recycle economic value into further African growth.

Strategic Thesis

Africa's economic opportunities are constrained not by the absence of resources or entrepreneurs, but by fragmented capital, infrastructure, markets, and institutional execution. A group that connects these layers can create stronger economics than isolated projects.

Core Principles

🎯

Execution Before Scale

Prove a small number of businesses before expanding the portfolio.

⚖️

Capital Matching

Use equity, debt, grants, PPPs and project finance according to the cash-flow profile.

🤝

Local Partnership

Enter countries through credible local operators, institutions and community relationships.

🔗

Value-Chain Integration

Prefer businesses that strengthen other businesses in the group.

🛡️

Governance First

Investor trust is an asset; controls must grow ahead of complexity.

🔄

Reinvestment

Use a disciplined share of profits to fund the next generation of growth.
