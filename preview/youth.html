How It Works

The AFRIH Pathway

From application to employment — a clear, structured journey for every young African who joins our programs.

1

Apply Online

Submit your application through our portal. Tell us about your interests, education, and career goals. No degree required for most programs — motivation matters most.

2

Assessment & Matching

We assess your aptitude and match you to the right program based on your strengths, interests, and local job market demand. Not everyone fits every program — we find the right fit.

3

Training & Certification

Complete your training program with hands-on instruction from industry practitioners. Earn certifications recognized by AFRIH companies and partner employers.

4

Apprenticeship / Internship

Put your skills to work immediately in a real AFRIH portfolio company or partner organization. Earn while you learn, build your network, and prove your value.

5

Job Placement or Startup

Graduate into full-time employment with AFRIH or our partners — or launch your own business with seed funding and mentorship from our entrepreneurship track.

6

Ongoing Support & Alumni Network

Join the AFRIH Alumni Network for continuing education, mentorship, job opportunities, and access to capital as you grow your career or business.

Job Creation

Where Our Graduates Work

AFRIH doesn't just train — we create the jobs. Every portfolio company is designed to employ young Africans.

01

Direct Employment

Hired directly into AFRIH portfolio companies — agriculture operations, energy plants, manufacturing facilities, logistics companies, technology startups, healthcare clinics, and hospitality ventures.

  • Agriculture & food processing jobs
  • Solar and energy plant technicians
  • Manufacturing and factory roles
  • Software development positions
  • Healthcare and clinic staff
  • Construction and infrastructure crews
02

Entrepreneurship

Start your own business with AFRIH support — seed funding from $5K-$50K, ongoing mentorship, market access through AFRIH networks, and preferred supplier status where possible.

  • Agribusiness startups
  • Tech and digital service companies
  • Renewable energy installers
  • Construction subcontractors
  • Logistics and transport services
  • Retail and e-commerce ventures
03

Partner Employment

We partner with local companies, multinationals, governments, and NGOs who hire our graduates. Our training is aligned to their hiring needs — creating a direct pipeline.

  • Corporate partner placements
  • Government and PPP programs
  • DFI-funded project roles
  • NGO and development programs
  • Multinational local operations
  • Cooperative enterprises
04

Gig & Freelance Economy

For digital skills graduates, we connect you to remote work platforms, freelance opportunities, and the global gig economy — earning international income from anywhere in Africa.

  • Remote software development
  • Virtual assistance & admin
  • Digital marketing & content
  • Data labeling & AI training
  • Online tutoring & teaching
  • E-commerce and digital trade
