How It Works

The AFRIH Pathway

From application to employment — a clear, structured journey for every young African who joins our programs.

1

Apply Online

Submit your application through our portal. Tell us about your interests, education, and career goals. No degree required for most programs — motivation matters most.

2

Assessment & Matching

We assess your aptitude and match you to the right program based on your strengths, interests, and local job market demand. Not everyone fits every program — we find the right fit.

3

Training & Certification

Complete your training program with hands-on instruction from industry practitioners. Earn certifications recognized by AFRIH companies and partner employers.

4

Apprenticeship / Internship

Put your skills to work immediately in a real AFRIH portfolio company or partner organization. Earn while you learn, build your network, and prove your value.

5

Job Placement or Startup

Graduate into full-time employment with AFRIH or our partners — or launch your own business with seed funding and mentorship from our entrepreneurship track.

6

Ongoing Support & Alumni Network

Join the AFRIH Alumni Network for continuing education, mentorship, job opportunities, and access to capital as you grow your career or business.

Eligibility

Who Can Apply

Our programs are designed to be accessible. We believe potential matters more than credentials.

🎟

Age Requirement

18 to 35 years old for most programs. Some technical and trade programs accept up to age 45. No upper age limit for entrepreneurship bootcamps.

🎓

Education

Most programs require no formal degree. Basic literacy is preferred but not mandatory for all tracks. We assess aptitude and motivation, not just certificates.

📍

Location

Open to young Africans across all regions. We operate training centers in urban and rural areas. Some programs offer remote/online participation options.

👧

Gender

All genders welcome. We actively encourage female participation with a 40% minimum target across all programs. Dedicated women's cohorts available in select locations.

🧑

Language

Programs delivered in English and French. Local language support available in select countries. Language training included where needed for technical programs.

💰

Cost

Free or heavily subsidized. No young African is turned away for inability to pay. Stipends available for low-income participants to cover transport and meals during training.

What You Get

What Graduates Receive

It's not just training — it's a complete support package designed to launch your career.

🎕

Certification

Industry-recognized certificate upon completion, accredited by national vocational training authorities and recognized by AFRIH partner employers.

💼

Job Placement

Dedicated placement officer to connect you with job opportunities in AFRIH companies and partner organizations. 80%+ placement target.

💰

Stipend Support

Living stipend during training for qualifying participants. Covers transport, meals, and basic expenses so you can focus on learning.

💻

Tools & Materials

All training materials, tools, and equipment provided. No hidden costs. Digital programs include loaner laptops where needed.

🤝

Mentorship

1-on-1 mentorship from industry professionals. Ongoing guidance during training and for 12 months after graduation.

🚀

Seed Funding

Entrepreneurship track graduates eligible for $5K-$50K seed funding to launch their own business. Plus ongoing business support.

🌍

Alumni Network

Lifetime access to AFRIH Alumni Network: job opportunities, continuing education, peer support, and access to capital as you grow.

📖

Continuing Education

Access to advanced courses and specializations after graduation. Stay current with industry trends and upgrade your skills over time.
