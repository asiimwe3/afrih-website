Governance & Risk

Trust Is a Financing Asset

Governance is designed before the organization becomes large — not after.

Governance Framework

Governance Bodies

Multiple oversight bodies ensure accountability at every level of the organization.

🏛️

Board of Directors

Strategy, oversight, CEO appointment, major transaction approval. Independent directors required above $100M AUM.

🔍

Audit & Risk Committee

Financial reporting, internal controls, risk management, external audit oversight. Reports directly to board.

📈

Investment Committee

Capital allocation, investment approvals, portfolio review, exit decisions. Includes external industry experts.

💰

Credit / Finance Committee

Debt approvals, guarantees, liquidity management, treasury policy. Independent risk officer has veto authority.

🌱

ESG / Impact Committee

Environmental, social, and impact standards. Screens investments, monitors portfolio ESG performance, publishes annual impact report.

🛡️

Internal Audit

Independent control testing across all entities. Reports to Audit Committee, not management. Full operational independence.

Risk Management

Risk Framework

Structured risk identification, measurement, mitigation, and monitoring across the platform.

Risk CategoryMitigation Strategy
Political / Country RiskDiversification across countries, local partnerships, political risk insurance (MIGA/ATI), PPP structures
Currency RiskRevenue-currency matching, hedging, local-currency financing where available, natural hedges via local sourcing
Execution RiskPhase-based capital deployment, milestone gates, experienced operating partners, stage-gate reviews
Market / Demand RiskDiversified sector exposure, demand validation before scaling, contracted revenue where possible
Regulatory RiskGovernment partnerships, regulatory compliance teams, sector-specific legal counsel, early engagement
Financial / LiquidityConservative leverage, liquidity reserves, staged capital calls, diversification of funding sources
ESG / ReputationalESG screening pre-investment, impact monitoring, community engagement, transparent reporting
