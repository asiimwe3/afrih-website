Business Model

How AFRIH Creates Value

A layered capital model matching maturity of capital to maturity of asset — with multiple revenue streams across the platform and human capital development at the core.

Revenue Streams

Multiple Revenue Engines

01

Operating Revenue

Revenue from portfolio companies — agriculture output, energy sales, manufacturing, logistics services, property rental, healthcare fees, education tuition, and tourism.

02

Capital Gains

Equity appreciation in portfolio companies and projects. Exit multiples on mature investments via trade sales, IPOs, or secondary buyouts.

03

Fund Management Fees

Management fees (1.5-2.5%) and carried interest (15-25%) on dedicated investment vehicles and sector funds.

04

Training Revenue

Tuition from paid training programs, corporate training contracts, government skilling partnerships, and certification fees. Cross-subsidized to keep programs accessible.

05

Financial Services

Interest income, trade finance margins, insurance premiums, and fintech transaction fees from financial sector platforms.

06

Strategic Dividends

Dividend income from mature portfolio companies — recycled into new investments and training programs per the reinvestment principle.

07

Advisory & Structuring

Project structuring fees, PPP advisory, capital arrangement services, and training consulting for external clients and partners.

08

Placement Fees

Employer fees for successful job placements from our training programs — connecting skilled graduates to companies that need them.

09

Alumni Network

Long-term value from alumni who become customers, suppliers, employees, and investors. The network effect compounds over time.

Fund Structure

Investment Vehicles

Multiple entry points for different investor profiles — from $10K diaspora tickets to $250M institutional commitments.

Growth Fund

$10M-$50M Tickets

Acquire and scale established companies across high-growth African sectors. Target IRR: 18-25%. 7-year fund life with 2-year extension option.

  • Sector agnostic within AFRIH focus areas
  • Majority or significant minority stakes
  • Active governance participation
  • Youth hiring commitments embedded
Infrastructure Fund

$50M-$250M

Energy, transport, industrial zones, and water infrastructure. Target IRR: 12-18%. 10-12 year fund life.

  • Project finance structuring
  • DFI co-investment
  • Government/PPP partnerships
  • Training programs for construction crews
Venture Fund

$1M-$10M Tickets

Technology and startups driving digital transformation. Target IRR: 25-40%+. 8-year fund life.

  • Seed through Series B
  • Africa-focused tech
  • Follow-on reserves
  • Youth-founded startup priority
Diaspora Platform

From $10K

Channel diaspora capital into productive African assets with transparent reporting and fractional participation.

  • Accessible minimum tickets
  • Quarterly impact reporting
  • Digital onboarding
  • Sponsor training scholarships
Impact Fund

From $50K

Dedicated to youth training, skilling programs, and job creation initiatives. Blended capital: commercial + concessional + grant. Target: 100K youth trained, 50K jobs created.

  • Training academy operations
  • Apprenticeship stipends
  • Entrepreneurship seed funding
  • Skills infrastructure (labs, workshops)
Education Fund

From $25K

Scholarships, vocational training infrastructure, digital learning platforms, and curriculum development partnerships with universities and training institutes.

  • Scholarship programs
  • Training center construction
  • Online learning platforms
  • University partnerships
